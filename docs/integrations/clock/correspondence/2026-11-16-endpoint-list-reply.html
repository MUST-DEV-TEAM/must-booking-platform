Below is the full list of endpoints our integration uses. "Now" means the code calls it today. "Next" means it is built or planned and we asked for the right ahead of use.

Reading (now)

APIRequestWhat we use it for
pms_apiGET /room_types, /roomsImporting room types and rooms
pms_apiGET /rates/, /rates/:idRates and the rate choice
pms_apiGET /rates_availabilityAvailability calendar and search
pms_apiGET /productsThe price of a stay
pms_apiGET /guests/search, /guests, /guests/:idMatching returning guests
pms_apiGET /bookings/, /bookings/:idReading and syncing reservations
pms_apiGET /bookings/:id/folios/Finding a booking's folios
base_apiGET /folios/:idReading a folio's balance and state
base_apiGET /folios/:id/credit_itemsReconciling payments

Writing (now)

APIRequestWhat we use it for
pms_apiPOST /bookings/Creating a reservation
pms_apiPUT /bookings/:idChanging or cancelling a reservation
pms_apiPOST /bookings/:id/folios/Creating the deposit folio for an online card payment
base_apiPOST /folios/:id/credit_itemsRecording a payment or a refund on the folio
base_apiDELETE /folios/:idVoiding the room folio when a booking is cancelled free of charge

Live updates (now)

APIRequestWhat we use it for
base_apiGET and POST /webhook_subscriptionCreating and reading the live-updates subscription once per hotel, when it is activated (done by our team by hand, not by a background job)

Next (right asked for ahead of use)

APIRequestWhat for
pms_apiGET /rate_plans/, /rate_plans/:idReal rate plans instead of our own hidden ones
pms_apiGET /rate_packages/Package rates
pms_apiDELETE /bookings/:id/booking_guests/:idRemoving a guest from a booking
base_apiGET /charges, /charge_templatesShowing and posting extra charges
base_apiGET /document_types, /payment_sub_typesReading document and payment types

We are constantly updating our system according to our clients' demands, so we may need to add more permissions in the future. We will always ask in advance, with a clear reason for each right, as we did this time. Because of that, we would like the standard profile to be easy to extend for all our accounts at once.
