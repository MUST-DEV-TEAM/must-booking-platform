## Cancellation, refunds and Clock settlement

Owner preference (2026-11-13): use standard hotel booking and accounting practices, not special handling that only makes one test booking look correct. Verify provider behavior and document requirements before implementation. For a fully refundable cancellation with no retained charges, the intended outcome is cancellation, confirmed refund, zero net retained payment and no remaining amount to collect. Preserve the original payment, refund and correction documents; do not erase history or force a displayed balance to zero while Clock remains unsettled.

Open findings and required improvements:

- **Unnecessary approval inside the free window.** Empire #15939 (`EBR-261112-2257-BPFV`) saved a 21-day free window, a 2027-03-30 deadline and automatic refunds enabled. Its stored financial check referred to booking version 5 while the pending request referred to version 6. This supports an outdated-check problem; the exact request-time failure was not conclusively logged. Refresh transient checks before requiring review, preserve genuine uncertainty safeguards, and record a clear reason for any fallback.
- **Closed deposit bills remain unsettled after refund.** #15939 is cancelled and its full ALL 16,280 test payment is recorded as refunded. Clock's stored records contain both the positive payment and negative refund, but the closed deposit bill retains ALL 16,280 in value/balance. #15935's open deposit bill instead has zero value/balance. Use documented Clock correction/Deposit Adjustment handling for closed documents; do not infer an advance-invoice type solely from a closed bill. No supported API for Deposit Adjustment has been established in the endpoint matrix.
- **Separate completion states.** Show cancellation, refund and Clock settlement independently, with the exact remaining staff action. Check guest cancellation, staff cancellation and cancellation initiated directly in Clock, using the original agreed terms; older bookings saved different automatic-refund settings.
- **Misleading money warning.** The cancelled-bill notice says Clock holds the original paid amount even after an equal refund. Show original payment, confirmed refund and net retained amount separately from outstanding document balances.
- **Overbroad or lingering reminders.** Refund posting always creates a Deposit Adjustment reminder, including for #15935, whose zero-balance warning resolved but whose adjustment reminder remains open. Verify whether a correction document is required/completed before creating or closing a reminder; zero balance alone does not prove document completion.
- **Unrelated recovery instructions.** The review card can show a generic matching-reservation/link-and-synchronize message for an already linked, cancelled booking with a financial issue. Show only instructions applicable to the current issue.
- **Pending refunds counted in displayed totals.** Booking projection queries sum REFUND rows without restricting them to completed status. Separate pending from completed refunds in totals and badges.
- **Quiet charge-cleanup failures.** Missing Clock rights during room-charge cleanup can produce only a log warning. Give staff an actionable booking-level notice whenever required cleanup cannot complete.

Validate free/late cancellation, open/closed deposit bills, partial/full/pending refunds, changed booking versions, staff permissions, duplicate requests and provider failures. Never repeat a cancellation or refund blindly to clear a notice. Canonical behavior and provider evidence: [booking and payments](../architecture/booking-and-payments.md), [Clock endpoint matrix](../integrations/clock/endpoint-matrix.md).

The first item is partly addressed: since 2026-11-16 (#494) Clock hydration raises the booking version only on a real change, and on 2026-11-18 the guest cancel path retries its money check once when a concurrent update interrupts it, and answers "reload" instead of sending the guest to staff review (branch `fix/guest-cancel-concurrent-update`). The other findings above are still open; this follow-up is not authorization for live provider actions, accounting corrections or deployment.
