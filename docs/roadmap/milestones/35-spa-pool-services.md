# Milestone 35: Spa, pool and other hotel services (future plan, not scheduled)

Status: **Idea recorded 2026-11-09. No tasks, no design, nothing to build yet.**

## Idea

The same approach for the rest of a hotel's services: the spa (treatments, therapists, time slots), the pool and similar places (entry, capacity, bookings), and any later service. Each would be a kind of venue ([Milestone 33](33-venues-restaurants.md)), separate from the hotel and connectable to it.

## Why it is only recorded

So that the venue model stays general enough. Nothing is scheduled; questions and tasks come after the restaurant ([33](33-venues-restaurants.md)) and beach ([34](34-beach-management.md)) work.
