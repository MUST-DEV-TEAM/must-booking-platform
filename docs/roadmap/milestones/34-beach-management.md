# Milestone 34: Beach management (future plan, not scheduled)

Status: **Idea recorded 2026-11-09. No tasks, no design, nothing to build yet.**

## Idea

Manage the beach the way a restaurant is managed: sunbeds, umbrellas and spots as a map, booked per day or half-day, with staff who see who sits where, deposits or prices, and a link to a hotel when the beach belongs to one. It would be another kind of venue ([Milestone 33](33-venues-restaurants.md)), reusing the same floor-map, staff and booking ideas.

## Why it is only recorded

The owner wants the plan known now so that earlier choices (the venue model, the floor map, staff roles) are not made in a way that blocks this later. Questions and tasks come when the restaurant work ([33](33-venues-restaurants.md)) is done.
