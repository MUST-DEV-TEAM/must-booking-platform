## A1 rules and contract (before implementation)

- Capture once per property-local calendar date, after 02:00 local time, next 365 stay dates including today. A repeatable-read transaction, property advisory lock and unique snapshot identity commit the entire capture or nothing; retries/concurrent workers cannot replace the first complete snapshot. Scheduled hourly sweep uses the existing scoped property-discovery/BullMQ pattern; each property job carries tenant/property. No replay for missed historical dates. Snapshot time is explicit and is an observation after 02:00, not an asserted midnight close. Retain snapshot dates today minus 799 days through today (800 dates). Stored source/timezone makes a source or timezone change an incompatible comparison.
- Snapshot parent records source/timezone/capture time; day facts store sold confirmed room-nights and gross available nights (nullable); currency facts store confirmed nightly room revenue as exact integer minor units (nullable when immutable pricing evidence is missing). Currency revenue never sums across currencies. No new pricing engine. Reuse Reports facts, supply and immutable-night primitives. No fake room-type/filter detail: property aggregate history is shown only for unfiltered compatible property scopes.
- Pickup compares the same future stay dates against actual snapshots 1/7/14/30 property days earlier; negative changes are allowed. Missing captured stay dates mean No history yet; unknown price evidence remains Unknown. Pace uses the same lead distance versus the comparable stay dates a year earlier, with leap-day clamping. Need complete compatible prior snapshots and current live stored facts; do not derive past state from present bookings. No endpoint to request a backdated capture. Existing Reports workspace/exports carry these derived summaries.
- Refund reason: nullable fixed enum guest_request/hotel_cancellation/duplicate_payment/overbooking/service_issue/goodwill/other. Existing POST payments/refunds accepts optional reasonCode; unknown values reject before processing. UI requires a selection; note remains optional. Persist with the existing refund transaction/audit and existing command idempotency; do not change amounts, provider invocations, retry/status logic. Automated or legacy refunds remain Unclassified unless explicitly captured.
- Charge category: nullable vat/tourist_tax/service/other on pricing_charges, optional at old API callers, captured on create and fixed for that charge identity. Existing charge versions and quote/booking snapshots are not rewritten. A new charge component copies the recorded category; Reports groups classified charge components and labels legacy/null Unclassified. Every mandatory charge continues to be excluded from room revenue; classification changes reporting only.
- Guest country: optional ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 allowlist (249 assigned codes; not inferred from phone/city). Persist when supplied at booking creation, present in guest directory/profile and staff booking address form. Property-accessible guest country edits use bookings.edit plus guests.view (no guests.edit capability exists), verified email, expected version and Idempotency-Key with atomic audit. Guest identity is tenant-owned in the existing model; updates must prove the guest has a booking in the selected property. No PMS update.
- New tables carry tenant_id and property_id, composite property FK, forced exact tenant/property RLS. Existing nullable columns retain their table's current scope. Migration additive/idempotent; rollback application-first, retain captured history/nullable fields, no destructive down migration.

## A2-A4 contract boundaries

Email reads/writes retain existing settings.manage unless the catalog already has a narrower applicable grant; verified email for mutations. Structured templates change wording only. Only registry-supported events are editable. Tests go only to the signed-in user's verified address or an explicitly authorized staff address; never sample guest addresses. No real send during verification: mock provider. Preserve reserved-address suppression and property hourly caps. Platform credentials/domain verification/transport controls remain read-only or hidden when unsupported, with exact gaps. Existing per-recipient privacy behavior is retained; do not add CC/BCC provider behavior merely for the design.

Notifications require current property membership and the registry's existing category capability; unknown types fail closed. Owner/admin breadth never bypasses tenant/property scope. Personal read receipts do not resolve needs-action state. Retention removes inbox records after 90 days, not the underlying booking/payment/audit evidence; unresolved items remain visible until resolved within that retention rule. Detail navigation is generated only for authorized existing routes.

Audit visibility follows D123: owners/admins property access; staff only existing explicitly permitted event families. Actor identity requires staff.view. Export uses the exact server-filtered authorized scope, UTF-8 BOM and formula protection, and audit must commit before content returns. Do not invent absent result/context/before/after; exclude secrets/tokens/provider credentials from details. No IP/location, hashes, attachments or review-note workflow. Exact A2-A4 route shapes will be recorded before their code.

## A1 implemented contract and evidence

Migration `20261019020000_report_capture` is additive and applied locally; executing the full SQL a second time passed. New snapshot/day/currency and guest retry-receipt tables use forced tenant/property RLS and composite scoped FKs. No business evidence backfilled. Technical guest capture_version starts at 1 for concurrency; it is not historical contact evidence. New assigned-country allowlist has 249 ISO codes; phone-specific XK is not an ISO 3166-1 assignment.

- Existing `POST /payments/refunds` accepts optional reasonCode from the fixed list; invalid values reject with 400. UI requires the reason. Stored on the REFUND row and existing audit, using the same command hash/transaction/provider calls/status/amount rules. Absent reason stays Unclassified, including automated refunds. Existing note behavior stays intact.
- Existing pricing charge create accepts category or null. Category is immutable even at database level; existing charges stay null and cannot be retroactively classified. Create a new charge for a different category. New quote nightly charge components carry category; old issued quotes/bookings are untouched. Reports charge detail adds category and uses Unclassified for older evidence, still excluding all mandatory charges from room-revenue KPIs.
- Booking guest payload accepts optional countryCode, normalized upper case and validated before processing. The existing local identity resolver captures it only when explicitly supplied; it makes no PMS guest update. Directory/booking read models expose countryCode and captureVersion. Staff booking address form and guest profile use the shared CountrySelect. `PATCH /guests/:guestId/country` requires bookings.edit AND guests.view, verified email, Idempotency-Key and `{countryCode:string|null,version:number}`; returns `{id,countryCode,captureVersion}`. The existing guest model is tenant-owned; selected-property booking linkage is required before editing. Property and guest row locks serialize writes; same request retry returns the receipt, changed intent/stale version is 409, inaccessible guest 404, audit failure rolls back. A country edit updates the shared local guest profile, never nationality or a historical snapshot. Reports origin counts unique confirmed staying guest identities with known current country; Unknown is excluded explicitly, not inferred.
- `reports.otb` BullMQ hourly scheduler discovers properties through the existing read-only scoped scheduler function; property jobs include tenant/property and local date. Captures after 02:00 local, 365 stay dates including today, once per property/day. Advisory serialization plus whole pure-database RepeatableRead retries handle competing workers. Header/day/currency facts, expiry cleanup and one `reports.otb.captured` audit commit atomically. Exact integer revenue comes from immutable nightly snapshots and the shared Reports gross supply formula; missing price is null, Clock supply is null. Revenue inserts are bounded chunks; at most 36,500 currency/day cells per snapshot, and existing Reports source caps reject entire overlarge captures without partial totals. Keep snapshot dates today-799 through today. No missed-day replay or capture HTTP endpoint.
- Reports Overview/Occupancy adds onTheBooksHistory to the existing workspace/exports. Unfiltered selected future dates within 365 days only; current live stored facts and historical daily captured observations carry as-of/capture times and basis. Pickup 1/7/14/30 allows negative changes. Pace compares against year-earlier snapshot date (Feb 29 clamps), then shifts stay dates by equal lead distance, preserving lead distance across leap years. Missing/incompatible/incomplete captures say No history yet. Money requires payments.view; currencies remain separate. No historical intraday reconstruction, booking amendments, filtered/type-level history or historical out-of-order classification.
